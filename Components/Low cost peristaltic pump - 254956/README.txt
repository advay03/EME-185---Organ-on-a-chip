Low cost peristaltic pump by Ralf on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:254956

Summary:
A peristaltic pump that uses two cheap 28BYJ-48 stepper motors.  It's designed for 6mm ODx4mm ID silicone tubing, and is able to pump ~700ml/hr, when the steppers is driven full step unipolar. Higher flow rates might be possible if driven bipolar.
